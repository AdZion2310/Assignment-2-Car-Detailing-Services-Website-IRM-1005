Ottawa’s hand car wash since 2012

A little care.
A big difference.

Bytown Shine began in 2012 with two wash bays, a pressure washer and a stubborn belief that a car should be washed by hand. Today our team looks after vehicles at three locations across Ottawa: Centretown, Orléans and Barrhaven.

From a 30-minute exterior wash to a full-day detail, we treat every vehicle the way we would want our own treated.

Our story

Built for Ottawa, one vehicle at a time.

Ottawa is hard on cars. Six months of road salt, spring pollen, summer bugs and fall sap mean a vehicle here needs more than a quick rinse. We built our packages around that calendar: proper salt removal in winter, decontamination and protection in spring, and a reliable hand wash the rest of the year.

We opened Orléans in 2016 and Barrhaven in 2020 so that an east-end or south-end commute would no longer mean a drive downtown for a good wash. Each location is run by a long-time member of the original Centretown crew.

How we work

Hand-washed. Every time.

No automated brushes, no recycled rinse water on your paint. Two-bucket hand washing, clean microfibre for every car, and pH-balanced products that are safe on ceramic coatings and vinyl wraps.
